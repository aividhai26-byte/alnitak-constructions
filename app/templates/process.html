How We Build

Our step-by-step methodology ensures that all designs comply with planning corporation rules and Vaastu before site excavation begins.

01

Phase 1: Planning

We begin with in-depth client consultations, site measurements, and structural analysis. Our draftsmen map out initial 2D floor layouts, room requirements, and optimize natural wind and light flows according to authentic Vaastu parameters.

02

Phase 2: Elevation & Design

Once the 2D layout is finalized, we render premium 3D front elevations. We specify exterior claddings, structural components, and balcony framing details, providing a complete photorealistic visualization of your villa or complex.

03

Phase 3: Approvals & Banking

We compile all blueprints and submit corporation files directly to municipal authorities for approvals. Concurrently, our financial liaison helps coordinate bank building loans, handling all file documentation requirements seamlessly.

04

Phase 4: Site Construction

Our experienced structural engineers take over the site. We handle excavation, RCC framework casting, brick masonry, wiring, tiling, modular woodworks, and finishes, ensuring strict compliance checks until key handover.
