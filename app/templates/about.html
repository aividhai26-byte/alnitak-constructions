Company Profile

Alnitak Constructions

Alnitak Constructions is an elite, full-service civil engineering and construction firm headquartered in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu. Registered under license TN-28-0078526 and led by owner Ramkumar Magheshwaran, our company stands as a beacon of architectural quality and integrity in the region.

We were founded with the mission to elevate local building practices. Over the past 12+ years, we have designed and built dozens of premium residential homes, villas, and commercial spaces. Our team manages every facet of the development cycle, including custom 2D/3D front elevations, site planning, building sanctions/corporation approvals, and banking arrangements.

We believe a building is not just a structure, but a lifetime asset. That is why we utilize high-grade concrete, perform meticulous structural load tests, and align every square foot with authentic Vaastu principles for light, wind, and prosperity.
