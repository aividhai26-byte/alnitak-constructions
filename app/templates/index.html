About Us
12+ Years of
Experience

Alnitak Constructions, founded by Ramkumar Magheshwaran in Tiruppur, has established itself as a premier name in the Tamil Nadu construction sector. We specialize in engineering high-end residential layouts, commercial properties, and custom elevation designs.

Our core philosophy revolves around structural strength, strict timelines, traditional Vaastu compliance, and complete procedural transparency. From blueprint planning to final completion, we manage all approval and banking workflows ourselves, delivering a hassle-free, premium experience.
